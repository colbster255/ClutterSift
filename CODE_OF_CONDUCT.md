# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Welcome different experience levels and perspectives. Give constructive feedback about work, not personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sharing private data are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or ban participants. Decisions should be proportional, consistent, and protect affected people. Report concerns using the repository's private reporting channel once configured; do not publish private details in an issue. Maintainers must establish a private contact before inviting public participation.
